Space Weather: Impacts on Life & Earth

How solar activity affects people, animals, plants, and Earth’s systems.

People Animals Plants Earth & Tech

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Impact on people

  • Earth’s surface: The atmosphere and Earth’s magnetic field provide significant protection, so the direct impact is mild.
  • Aviation: On polar flight routes, exposure to radiation increases, which can pose a risk to crews and frequent passengers.
  • Astronauts: In space (outside the magnetosphere), the risk is high because they are directly exposed to charged particles, which can cause cellular damage and increase cancer risks.

🐦 Impact on animals

  • Magnetic navigators: Animals such as migratory birds, sea turtles, and some fish use Earth’s magnetic field to orient themselves. During geomagnetic storms, they can become disoriented.
  • General fauna: On the surface, the impact is low, since they are protected just like humans by the atmosphere and magnetic field.

🌱 Impact on flora (plants)

  • Direct impact: Practically none at the surface, because plants are also protected by the atmosphere.
  • Indirect impact: It may appear if space weather damages power grids, satellites, or systems that support agriculture (e.g., irrigation systems, agricultural GPS). This indirectly affects food production.

🌍 Impact on planet Earth (systems)

Magnetosphere: It is the first to take the hit. A strong storm can distort auroras and trigger geomagnetic storms.

Technology

  • Satellites: Electronic failures and loss of GPS accuracy.
  • Power grids: Blackouts from currents induced in transformers.
  • Communications: Radio interruptions and satellite link issues.

Auroras (northern and southern lights): Although a “beautiful” effect, they are the visible sign that solar particles are interacting with the atmosphere.
